## What it is

Tacit-to-Technical Transduction™ (TTT) explains how practitioner pattern intelligence becomes technical or system architecture without losing the source.

Most expert practice stays tacit — the practitioner knows what to do and cannot fully say why. TTT names the process by which that knowledge, formed under real pressure over years, becomes documented architecture, frameworks, and systems other people can use.

## Position in the architecture

TTT runs after the rest of the primary stack, not alongside it. It converts what NAM opened.

```
Neuroformation™ (why formation happens)
    ↓
Elevation Grid™ (where the fracture is)
    ↓
Neural Access Method™ (opens access)
    ↓
TTT (converts what was accessed into architecture)
```

**Neural Access Method™ impacts TTT directly.** NAM is the transmission protocol that opens access to the formation living in the practitioner. Without NAM opening that access, there is nothing for TTT to transduce. NAM does not produce TTT's output — but TTT cannot run without what NAM opens.

**Membership vs. current output route — two different facts.** TTT is a shared NFA-family object by architecture and identity, the same as every other component on this page. Separately, and not in tension with that: TTT's *current forward-transduction* output routes to VGS only. Once practitioner cognition is transduced into technical architecture, that output becomes applied AI frameworks and published research — not coaching content, not formation content. That's a statement about where forward output goes today, not a claim that TTT itself belongs to VGS.

## What it is not

Not a claim that tacit knowledge is fully reducible to explicit rules. Some of it isn't. TTT describes what survives the transfer, not a promise that nothing is lost.

## Why it matters here

Decades of applied coaching practice, much of it never written down, became a named and documented structure through this process. Neuroformation™ itself is the clearest case — discovered inside practice that already worked, then named March 14, 2026 and published as [Neuroformation™ v1.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19197818).

TTT is also the mechanism by which ASFA — the private research layer underneath NFA — hardens into something public. Practitioner cognition tested in ASFA becomes documented architecture through the same conversion this page describes.
