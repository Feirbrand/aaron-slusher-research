## What it is

Tacit-to-Technical Transduction™ (TTT) is a two-way transduction framework. Forward, it explains how practitioner pattern intelligence becomes technical or system architecture without losing the source. In reverse, it translates technical or AI-system behavior into pattern language an operator can read and test.

Most expert practice stays tacit — the practitioner knows what to do and cannot fully say why. TTT names the process by which that knowledge, formed under real pressure over years, becomes documented architecture, frameworks, and systems other people can use.

**Forward transduction:** formed practitioner intelligence → explicit, testable architecture.

**Reverse transduction:** technical or AI-system behavior → operator-readable candidate pattern language, for testing. Reverse output is a candidate to test, not a claim about mechanism. It does not mean "this behavior looks like pressure pattern X, so X is the mechanism." It means observed technical behavior, then a candidate pattern, then a formal mapping, a test, evidence, and a claim status.

## Position in the architecture

TTT's place under NFA describes where it is organized, not the order in which things happen. It can operate very early — transducing practitioner pattern intelligence is often how architecture gets formalized in the first place — and it does not run last.

It works in two modes:

- **Origin mode.** A practitioner's formed judgment is transduced into explicit candidate architecture. This is often how material across the NFA family was first formalized, and this mode does not wait on NAM.
- **Application mode.** In a live session, Neural Access Method™ (NAM) opens access to what is reachable in that moment, and TTT formalizes what was accessed. NAM impacts TTT directly in this mode: without access established in the session, there is nothing in that moment to transduce.

TTT converts. NAM delivers.

**Routing and scope — two different facts.** TTT is a shared NFA-family object by architecture and identity, the same as every other component on this page. Its application scope is all three expressions of the work — human performance, AI systems, and formation work. Separately, its technical research and implementation route through VGS: applied AI frameworks, published research, the codex. That describes where technical research goes, not who TTT belongs to.

## What it is not

Not a claim that tacit knowledge is fully reducible to explicit rules. Some of it isn't. TTT describes what survives the transfer, not a promise that nothing is lost.

Not proof by analogy. A practitioner's analogy can act as a translation seed — a hypothesis-generating bridge into a technical formalization. It is not itself evidence. The analogy is the bridge; the framework is the formalization; evidence decides what survives.

## Why it matters here

Decades of applied coaching practice, much of it never written down, became a named and documented structure through this process. Neuroformation™ itself is the clearest case — discovered inside practice that already worked, then named March 14, 2026 and published as [Neuroformation™ v1.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19197818).

ASFA investigates and hardens candidate mechanisms; surviving material may be promoted into NFA, the public architecture, which also organizes established work that predates ASFA. TTT is one of the defined mechanisms in that body of work.
