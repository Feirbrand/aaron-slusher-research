## What it is

The Parate Process™ names the pattern by which capability — physical, cognitive, relational, or systemic — becomes temporarily inaccessible under pressure without being lost, and the structured response that clears the way back to it.

Capability that is out of reach is not capability that is gone. Parate is the work of clearing and preparing the pathway.

## The sequence

**Out of Reach → Pathway → Unlock → Execute**

## Why the name

*Parate* is Latin — the imperative of *parare*, "to prepare, make ready." Its Spanish descendant *párate* means "stop" and "stand up." Same root, two documented branches, and each one names a real moment in the process — and they are sequential, not simultaneous:

1. **Párate** (Spanish) — interrupt the cascade. Regain footing. Stop letting pressure drive.
2. **Parate** (Latin) — clear the obstruction. Prepare the pathway back to what is still there.

Control has to be interrupted before the way can be prepared. That order doesn't reverse.

It appears in the Latin Vulgate at Isaiah 40:3, Matthew 3:3, and Mark 1:3 — *"Parate viam Domini,"* prepare the way of the Lord. It is a word, not a name or a place.

The word was chosen, not coined.

## Lineage

Parate descends from an earlier working name, Pressure Access Method (PAM), through a refactor — not a simple rename. Same underlying job, same disclosure-ladder philosophy: architecture fixed, vocabulary flexes.

## Where it sits

Parate does one job: it clears a blocked pathway. It is what comes before [Neural Access Method™](../neural-access-method/) can transmit anything through that pathway, and before [Ground. Ascent. Altitude.™](../ground-ascent-altitude/) can build anything on it.

> The Parate Process™ clears and prepares the pathway.
> Ground. Ascent. Altitude.™ builds what must hold.

Parate runs under any application built on this architecture — not exclusive to a single product or cohort.

Like every NFA component, it operates inside adaptive cognition infrastructure (ACI) — the domain descriptor for how human, organizational, and human-AI systems preserve coherence and execution under load.

**Correct form:** The Parate Process™. Never "Parate Method" — *Method* belongs to Neural Access Method™.

**Note:** *The Parate Process* is also the name of Aaron Slusher's newsletter. Same brand, different register — the newsletter carries this methodology's name; it is not the methodology itself.
