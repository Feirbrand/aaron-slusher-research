# State-Gated Access

**Status:** Locked
**NFA Layer:** Five-Layer Substrate / L1–L2
**Mechanism ID:** MECH-3

## What it explains

How autonomic state determines which cognitive and emotional routes are available.

## Definition

Autonomic state is the gate determining which cognitive and emotional routes are reachable at a given moment. The same skill, knowledge, or intention can be fully present and functionally unreachable — not from deficit, but because the door to it is closed by current state. As arousal escalates, available channels narrow in a predictable, state-dependent way.

## Origin and provenance

Observed directly in coaching: athletes and clients possessing a skill in low-stakes conditions losing access to it under real pressure, with no loss of the underlying capability. Formalized as a distinct mechanism from Signal-to-Constraint Conversion — the classification of a signal and the gating of access to respond to it are separate steps.

## Evidence base

- Klinger (2001, NIJ, *Police Responses to Officer-Involved Shootings*) — real-world acute-stress perceptual narrowing: tunnel vision, diminished/altered sound perception, and altered time perception under genuine shooting conditions.
- Schoofs, Preuß & Wolf (2008, *Psychoneuroendocrinology*, DOI 10.1016/j.psyneuen.2008.02.004) — experimentally induced psychosocial stress impaired working-memory performance.
- Qin et al. (2009, *Biological Psychiatry*, DOI 10.1016/j.biopsych.2009.03.006) — acute psychological stress reduced working-memory-related dorsolateral prefrontal cortex activity.
- Arnsten (2015, *Nature Neuroscience*) — mechanistic review: acute uncontrollable stress weakens prefrontal networks and biases behavior toward more reflexive circuitry.

Together these give a complete evidence chain — real-world observation → controlled human experiment → neural evidence → mechanistic review — not a single-level claim.

**Adjacent/application support, not core evidence:** Granero-Gallegos et al. (2021) on HRV-guided training and Boukhris et al. (2025) on structured napping remain relevant to readiness and recovery application, but do not themselves establish that state gates cognitive access — that claim rests on the four sources above. A cross-substrate structural parallel (Torque, MI Arsenal) is a separate, lower-strength comparison, not counted as core evidentiary support.

## Claim ceiling

**Warranted:** autonomic state gates route availability, testable and observed across acute (auditory exclusion) and trainable-readiness (multi-marker gating) contexts.

**Not warranted:** precise prediction of which specific channel closes first for a given individual, or a validated unified formula combining HRV/breath-hold/grip into one score — the multi-marker approach has support; the specific triad as a package does not yet.

## Boundary conditions

Applies under genuine acute or accumulating physiological load. Does not claim all routes close uniformly — highly automatized channels can remain open under load that closes others (see Witness / Metacognitive Regulation). Would be undermined by evidence that channel availability under load is better predicted by trait than by state.

## How it appears in practice

**Acute stress:** auditory exclusion/tunnel vision in high-stakes incidents. **Trainable readiness:** multi-marker gating before high-intensity work. **AI systems:** Torque's coherence-threshold gating, structural parallel only. Practitioner-floor detail not yet included — known gap.

## Related mechanisms

Signal-to-Constraint Conversion — a classified signal is what triggers the state that gates access. Witness / Metacognitive Regulation — automaticity determines whether a closed gate disrupts execution or leaves it untouched.
