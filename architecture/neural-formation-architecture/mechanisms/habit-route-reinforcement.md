# Habit Route Reinforcement

**Status:** Locked
**NFA Layer:** —
**Mechanism ID:** MECH-5

## What it explains

How repetition consolidates routes into default paths.

## Definition

A route used repeatedly becomes the default — not because it was chosen as best, but because repetition itself does the consolidating. This applies to physical movement patterns, cognitive habits, and compensation strategies alike: whatever gets used enough becomes what happens automatically, independent of whether it's still the right choice.

## Evidence base

- Lally et al. (2010, *European Journal of Social Psychology*, DOI 10.1002/ejsp.674) — real-world study of habit formation through repetition, directly measuring automaticity development over time.
- Wood & Rünger (2016, *Annual Review of Psychology*, DOI 10.1146/annurev-psych-122414-033417) — review: repeated responses become efficient, default habitual responses, largely independent of continued deliberate intent.
- Kleim & Jones (2008, DOI 10.1044/1092-4388(2008/018)) — review of experience-dependent neural plasticity, including rehabilitation contexts directly relevant to compensation-pattern hardening.
- Graybiel (2008, *Annual Review of Neuroscience*, DOI 10.1146/annurev.neuro.29.051605.112851) — review of the neural habit circuitry underlying default-path consolidation.

Pain-science writing on chronic pain duration and neural entrainment (commonly associated with Paul Ingraham) is retained as practitioner/application-level explanation for the compensation-pattern context specifically — secondary to the core evidence above, not the anchor for it.

## Claim ceiling

**Warranted:** repetition consolidates routes into default, largely automatic paths — well-supported across behavioral, social-psychological, and neuroscientific literature.

**Not warranted:** a precise, validated timeline for exactly when a given pattern becomes fully default ("reps" or "days" framings are coaching heuristics, not established constants).

## Boundary conditions

Applies to any repeated route — physical, cognitive, or compensatory. Would be undermined if pattern resistance to change showed no relationship to duration/repetition at all.

## How it appears in practice

Chronic compensation patterns in rehabilitation and coaching contexts — practitioner-floor detail not yet included.

## Related mechanisms

Coached Evidence Loop — repeated coached-evidence loops are one route by which a new pattern becomes default. False Attractor Formation — a related but distinct mechanism.
