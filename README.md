# Aaron M. Slusher — Independent Research Archive

This repository is the central research archive for the independent work of Aaron M. Slusher.

It preserves the public research spine behind methodology, adaptive systems, human performance, AI resilience, formation architecture, and cross-domain studies that may originate inside or outside any single brand.

The brands apply the work. This repository preserves the spine — and it's the center of a wider web of repos: from here, the work fans out to applied repos like ValorGrid Solutions' `synoeticos-public`, and future brand-specific repos, each of which may fan out further to its own hubs. Start here for the overview, and follow the links for depth.

## The Work

This is where the research lands when it's bigger than any one brand — before it becomes coaching language, AI-resilience architecture, formation work, or public methodology, and sometimes before it's ready to be public at all.

What ties it together is adaptive systems under pressure: how they form, where they fracture, and what it actually takes to get them working again from whatever is left standing.

Achieve Peak Performance is the established human-performance application of the work. ValorGrid Solutions and Foundations & Fire are growing research interests in AI/systems and faith formation respectively, with their own public footprints developing over time.

Other research lines don't belong to any one brand — origin studies, cross-domain synthesis, and underlying research that stays outside this public repository until it's ready.

This isn't a product catalog. It's the research spine everything else hangs off of.

## Origin Note

1999 marks the first formal certification on record — not the origin of the underlying pattern or the beginning of the methodology.

The named frameworks were formalized much later, as recurring patterns across practice, recovery, performance, AI systems, and formation research became clear enough to document.

Some underlying research substrates remain unpublished for safety, IP, and development reasons.
